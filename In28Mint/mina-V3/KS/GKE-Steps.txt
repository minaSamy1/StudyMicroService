9- AutoScaling  number of Pods based usage
    kubectl autoscale deployment hello-world-rest-api --max=10 --cpu-percent=70

, Kubernetes will monitor the CPU usage of your pods in the hello-world-rest-api deployment,
 and it will automatically increase or decrease the number of pods to maintain a target CPU usage of 70%.

         - name: CURRENCY_EXCHANGE_URI
